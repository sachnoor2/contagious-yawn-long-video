TITLE: Tumhari Yawn Dusron Ko Kyun Lagti Hai?

HOOK:
Kisi ko yawn karte dekho… aur kuch seconds baad tum khud yawn karne lagte ho. Kya yawn hawa mein spread hoti hai?

Ek yawn contagious lagti hai, lekin iska matlab ye nahi ki koi virus transfer ho raha hai. Yawn dekhna, sunna, ya kabhi-kabhi sirf uske baare mein padhna bhi brain ke social circuits ko activate kar sakta hai.

Sabse pehle, tumhara brain saamne wale ka action automatically simulate karta hai. Is process mein superior temporal sulcus aur mirror-related networks doosre insaan ke expression aur movement ko read karte hain. Jab brain yawn ka pattern recognise karta hai, motor system us pattern ko internally rehearse kar sakta hai—jaise brain ne movement ka shortcut save kar rakha ho.

Lekin yawn sirf imitation nahi hai. Yawning sleepiness, boredom, stress, temperature change, aur alertness transition se linked hai. Jab tum kisi ko yawn karte dekhte ho, tumhara brain us situation ko apni body state ke saath compare karta hai. Agar tum already tired ho, threshold cross karna aur easy ho jaata hai.

Is effect mein attention aur empathy bhi role play kar sakte hain. Close friends aur family ke yawns kabhi strangers ke comparison mein zyada contagious feel hote hain. Lekin ye universal rule nahi hai. Har yawn copy nahi hoti, aur iska matlab ye bhi nahi ki jo person yawn copy nahi karta usmein empathy nahi hai. Sleep, focus, personality, aur context sab response ko change karte hain.

Ek aur myth: yawn ka main kaam brain ko thanda karna proven fact nahi hai. Temperature regulation ek possible explanation hai, lekin scientists abhi bhi yawning ke exact purpose par research kar rahe hain. Yawn likely ek multi-purpose body signal hai—sleep-wake transition, arousal, aur social communication ka mix.

Toh next time kisi meeting mein yawn chain shuru ho, panic mat karna. Tumhara brain bas ek visible body signal ko detect karke apni state se match kar raha hai. Yawn contagious nahi—tumhara social brain highly responsive hai.

END CARD: One yawn. Many brains. Science samajh aayi?
